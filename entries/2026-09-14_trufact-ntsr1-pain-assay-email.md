## Full summary (paper)

TRU-FACT registers the **same individual cells** from in vivo Ca²⁺ imaging onto postmortem spatial biology (HCR-FISH and/or MERFISH), plus optional projection barcodes. Three pieces: (1) optomechanical handling so tissue is cut parallel to the in vivo planes; (2) Soma-print, a graph of each cell’s neighbors, instead of image-to-image warping; (3) a per-cell posterior probability of a correct match. Validated in cortex and deep brain, head-fixed and freely moving (including Mini-2P), with low- and high-plex transcriptomics. Example in the paper: skilled reaching, IT/ET and projection-defined motor cortex cells.

This is **not** a substitute for functional analysis. It is how you attach a molecular / projection label to an already-defined in vivo cell ID.
