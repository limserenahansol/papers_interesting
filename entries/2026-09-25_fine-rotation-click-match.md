# Fine rotation in the in vivo / confocal click matcher

## User message that triggered this

Fine rotation, not only 90 and 180 degree steps.

## Paper IDs / links

- Wang, Jiang, Sun et al., *bioRxiv* 2026, TRU-FACT. Affine pre-alignment is still manual points before Soma-print. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.28.719500

## Full summary

The click matcher now rotates either image by 1, 5, 15, or 90 degrees. `5` changes the step. `i`/`I` turn the in vivo image left/right, `c`/`C` turn the confocal image. Points already clicked move with that image. The window opens at a 1 degree step, with the in vivo view still starting 90 degrees left from the raw z-stack.
