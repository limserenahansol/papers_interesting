# Rotated in vivo vs confocal slices 16 and 26

## User message that triggered this

Rotate the in vivo images 90 degrees left. Confocal slice 26 looks like the best match for cell video 2 (plane 2). Is slice ~16 plane 1?

## Paper IDs / links

- Wang, Jiang, Sun et al., *bioRxiv* 2026, TRU-FACT. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.28.719500

## Full summary

In vivo pain averages were rotated 90° counterclockwise and compared with confocal GCaMP at the same µm scale. Slice 26 remains the user's eye match for video 2. A translation-only normalized cross-correlation does not confirm it (peak 0.22; slice 34 is 0.27). For video 1, slice 16 is the highest score (0.26) but slices 15–22 are within 0.01, so “16-ish” is the right neighborhood, not a locked plane. Slices 16 and 26 are 30 µm apart; the two ETL planes were 50 µm apart. Affine and Soma-print were not run.
