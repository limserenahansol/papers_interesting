# Horizontal and vertical flip in the click matcher

## User message that triggered this

Add vertical and horizontal flip, in addition to fine rotation.

## Paper IDs / links

- Wang, Jiang, Sun et al., *bioRxiv* 2026, TRU-FACT. https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.04.28.719500

## Full summary

The click matcher can now mirror either panel. `h` flips in vivo left-right, `H` flips confocal left-right, `j` flips in vivo up-down, `J` flips confocal up-down. Pressing the same key again restores the image. Clicked points move with the flip. Flip flags are written into the saved pair table.
